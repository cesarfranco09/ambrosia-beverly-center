Our Story

A Taste of the
Mediterranean in L.A.

In Greek myth, ambrosia was the food of the gods on Mount Olympus. We named our table after it.

Our kitchen cooks where Greece and Italy meet. Saganaki comes to the table hot and golden. Fresh tagliatelle is tossed with lobster. Souvlaki is grilled over high heat, and branzino is finished with lemon and good olive oil. We bake the pastry and whisk the panna cotta in-house, and the honey on your waffle is Greek.

You can stop in for a freddo cappuccino between shops, sit down to a long lunch, catch happy hour after work, or come for dinner with family. Ambrosia is our corner of the Aegean in the middle of Los Angeles.
